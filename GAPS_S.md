Port-side divergences of mechanisms written here (documented at each site):

- `compat::batch_s_set_tooltip_for_next_frame`: a mod widget's tooltip is parked and handed to the frame's deferred
  slot (`Gui#extractRenderState`'s `deferred_chat_tooltip`) at the next frame's HEAD - one frame late, default style.
- `compat::batch_s_on_container_screen_remove`: `ScreenEvents.remove` fires at `AbstractContainerScreen#onClose` HEAD
  (the port removes a vanilla container screen only through `on_close_through_the_player`).
- `compat::batch_s_register_modify_command`: a changed command is cancelled and re-sent as the next client task.
- `compat::batch_s_register_after_block_break`: listeners run as the next client task (the caller holds the player).
- `END_LEVEL_TICK` (mob glow, lower sensitivity) is `END_CLIENT_TICK` with a level guard (`ClientLevel.tick` is inline
  in `Minecraft.tick`, not a hooked method).
- The accessory helper's `screen.setX(x)` from the tab button lands at the screen's next `extractRenderState` HEAD.
